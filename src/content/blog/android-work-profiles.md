Android work profiles provide a separate space for work-managed apps and data on a device. They are designed to keep an organization's apps and information distinct from the personal side of the phone.

## How the separation works

Apps installed in a work profile are separate app instances from their personal-profile counterparts. For example, a messaging app can have one personal account and a different work account.

The work profile may have its own:

- App installations and app data
- Managed account and configuration
- Notifications and sharing restrictions
- Policies controlled by an organization

A briefcase badge commonly identifies work-profile apps in the launcher.

## Pausing the work profile

Many Android devices let you pause a work profile from Quick Settings or the app drawer. Pausing it generally stops work apps from running and sending notifications until the profile is resumed.

The exact controls depend on the Android version and the device's management setup.

## Removing a work profile

If the profile was created by an app such as a device-policy controller, remove it through the supported work-profile or management-app settings. Some organization-managed devices restrict removal, and removing a profile deletes its local work-profile data.

Back up anything you are allowed to keep before removing a profile. If the device is managed by an employer or school, check its policy first.

## Personal and work data

A work profile is a separation feature, not a guarantee that every activity is private from an organization. Administrators may manage work apps, accounts and data under their policies. Review the management notice and understand what your organization can control.

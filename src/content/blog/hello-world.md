Welcome. This blog is a notebook for the technical things worth documenting: the setup that finally worked, the command that solved a problem, and the lessons learned along the way.

The focus is practical rather than theoretical. Posts will cover Android customization, Linux and ChromeOS experiments, developer tools, and useful open-source software.

## What you'll find here

- **Android:** ADB, app troubleshooting, customization and device maintenance.
- **Reverse engineering:** Notes on inspecting APK files and understanding Android application structure.
- **Linux and ChromeOS:** Setup notes, compatibility, recovery and troubleshooting.
- **Developer tools:** Small workflows that make everyday technical work easier.

Posts are written as working notes. Commands and procedures should always be checked against the device, operating-system version and tool documentation before use.
